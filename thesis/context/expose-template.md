# Tasty and Gluten-Free Bamboo Fiber Bread

## Introduction

Making bread is an age-old art. It has been practiced since the middle ages and billions of people enjoy it daily. However, there is one problem: A significant number of people are allergic to the Gluten [1] protein that is found in wheat products like bread. This stops a significant amount of people from enjoying the awesomeness that is bread [2].

In my thesis I want to contribute to approaching this problem. My theory is that we can make gluten-free bread by substituting wheat flour with bamboo fiber. I express this in the following research hypothesis:

> **Research Hypothesis:** Bread made from bamboo fiber tastes as good as bread made with wheat flour.

In my bachelor thesis I want to evaluate this hypothesis throughout a series of experiments.

---

## Approach and Preliminary Results

To validate my hypothesis I will run taste tests. In each test run a person will receive a bread baked with wheat-flour and one bread baked with bamboo flour. They then have to indicate which one tastes better. During the test procedure neither the test instructor nor the test subject will know which bread is which.

In order to have these tests we need to develop a bread-baking recipe based on bamboo fiber. Our first approach will be to substitute wheat flour with bamboo fiber one to one. But we assume that this recipe can be improved upon. I anticipate this task to require a lot of experimentation, iteration and trial and error. The development of this recipe will be part of my capstone project. It will follow a similar approach as described in [3].

---

## Preliminary Structure

My thesis will consist of the following chapters (plus an abstract):

1. **Introduction** — This chapter will summarize the domain and problem.
2. **State of the Art** — This chapter will list other approaches towards making gluten-free bread. I will also include any bamboo-fiber based recipes and approaches for substituting wheat-flour with other ingredients I come across during literature research.
3. **Research Hypothesis** — In this short chapter I will summarize my research hypothesis and elaborate on the consequences of validating / invalidating it.
4. **Bamboo-Bread Recipe** — Here I will report on the outcome of my bachelor thesis in the form of the bamboo bread recipe that will be used in the tests performed.
5. **Taste Test Procedure** — Here I will report on the exact procedure of our taste tests.
6. **Test Results** — I will report on the test results achieved and interpret them in context of my hypothesis.
7. **Discussion** — Discuss results in the context of state of the art literature.
8. **Conclusion** — I will give a short conclusion and point out future work that could be done based on the results I achieved.
