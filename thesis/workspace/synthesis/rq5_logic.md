# RQ5 — Synthesis

Open-weight vs API-only is a weak grouping for LLMorpheus outcomes. No metric shows a statistically significant category difference (all p > 0.38; negligible effect sizes). The only directional pattern is economic: open-weight models cost less per survivor, but individual models within each category span wide quality and stability ranges (cf. Qwen vs Llama in RQ1/RQ2).

**Literature link:** Quality assurance of LLM-generated code often frames deployment model as a first-order decision; our results suggest *specific model identity* matters more than license category for mutation-testing use.

**Practitioner takeaway:** Select models by empirical profile (score, stability, equivalence, cost) rather than open-weight vs API-only labels. Hybrid models (DeepSeek) sit between categories on cost and equivalence.
