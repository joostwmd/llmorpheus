# RQ4 — Synthesis

Cost spans two orders of magnitude ($0.04–$8.93 per six-package run). Per-token cheapness does not imply cost efficiency: invalid and duplicate mutants waste spend (Claude Haiku: 32% invalid). Cost per non-equivalent survivor separates value leaders (Llama 8B, Llama 70B) from premium models (Claude Sonnet). Qwen combines top mutation score with mid-tier cost — a quality–cost trade-off, not a Pareto dominance.

**Literature link:** Wang et al. (2025) emphasize economic factors in LLM mutation testing; our Pareto analysis operationalizes this for practitioner model choice.

**Practitioner takeaway:** Budget-constrained teams should prioritize cost per non-equivalent survivor over headline mutation score or per-token price.
